      Method and sources

      How to read these numbers

      What these numbers count

      These are the people in prison at the end of the year (a stock), not the year’s admissions or convictions. People serving long sentences weigh more. The offences are those charged, including for people awaiting trial: one person can have more than one. Minors are excluded: they are held in juvenile penal institutions.

      Prisoners and residents are not the same population

      Foreign residents are those on the population register on 1 January. Prisoners also include people without a residence permit or passing through Italy, who do not appear among residents: this is why the share among prisoners and the share among residents cannot be compared like for like.

      Citizenships and groups

      ISTAT lists individually only the countries with the most prisoners; the others are grouped by continent or area. The groups follow the source’s classification.
